# Crystal Ball Approvals — Operator Policy Note

## Purpose
Approvals in Crystal Ball Command Centre gate operational content before it reaches live digital-twin sites. An operator reviews each item for completeness, safety relevance, and correct classification before Approve, Request Changes, or Escalate.

## Review order
1. Safety-related PDFs and equipment/sensor specs always come first when urgency is high.
2. Onboarding folders and checklists come next — incomplete onboarding blocks new site activation.
3. Demo videos and spatial maps are reviewed after safety and onboarding unless a site go-live is scheduled within 24 hours.
4. When two items share the same urgency, prefer the earlier submission timestamp.

## How to review an item
Open the item preview. Confirm the submitter is an authorised HelpAdmin. Check that the title matches the asset type (Folder / Demo Video / PDF / Image). For PDFs and specs, verify version and that no redacted safety limits are missing. For videos, spot-check the first 30 seconds and the labelled patrol zone. For images/maps, confirm camera IDs align with the zone layout legend.

## Actions
- **Approve** — content is complete, correctly typed, and safe for twin publication.
- **Request Changes** — missing metadata, wrong type, or incomplete checklist; leave a short note naming the gap.
- **Escalate** — safety ambiguity, unknown submitter, or conflicting sensor limits. Escalations route to the Shift Lead within 15 minutes during business hours.

## SLA
High-urgency items should be actioned within 4 hours of submission. Medium within one business day. Low within three business days. Items older than the SLA appear with a “stale” hint in the assistant summary.

## Language & tone
Speak to operators in plain operational English. Prefer short sentences. Never invent policy that is not in this note. If a question is outside this document, say so and suggest escalating to Shift Lead.
